Things to Consider

Ideally, you will want an idea of your budget from month to month. Continue tracking for several months to see how your spending habits change. Think about major events or purchases throughout the year and how they might affect your budget. For example, you might need to plan for new shoes or clothes before a new school year starts or have extra funds set aside for holiday gifts. Consider adjusting individual categorys to account for these changes. One option to cover these variable or intermittant expenses is to plan for an annual total, divide that total by 12 (or less depending on your timeline) and then use that number in your monthly budget to set aside savings throughout the year. This will help ensure you will have funds to cover those expenses when you need them.
