What to look for

There are many reputable professionals such as certified financial advisors who can provide sound financial advice. While many people may give sound advice to the best of their abilities, professionals with these certifications are highly educated on finanical topics and bound by a code of conduct to act as fiduciaries. This means they are required to give recommendations based on your best interest or risk losing their certifications. Not everyone you might find online is bound by this same duty.

There are also many free resources available to help you improve your finanical literacy. For example, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission provides a variety of guides and worksheets at consumer.gov, including the budget worksheet that is the basis of this project.
